## Steps
1. Confirm the store name, the lane and the device affected.
2. Check power, cables and network lights at the device.
3. Restart the device following the documented procedure.
4. Raise a vendor call if there is a hardware fault and record the reference.
## Escalate when
- More than one lane is down.
- The store cannot take payments.
