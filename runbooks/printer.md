## Steps
1. Check the printer is powered on, has paper and shows no error.
2. Confirm the printer is online and on the same network as the user.
3. Clear the print queue and restart the print spooler on the user's device.
4. Remove and re-add the printer if the problem remains.
## Escalate when
- The printer shows a hardware fault.
- Several users are affected, which may point to the print server.
