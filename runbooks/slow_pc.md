## Steps
1. Ask when the problem started and whether anything was installed or updated.
2. Check uptime, disk space and memory use on the device.
3. Install pending updates and restart the device.
4. Rebuild or replace the device if the problem repeats.
## Escalate when
- The device shows a blue screen repeatedly.
- The disk reports errors.
